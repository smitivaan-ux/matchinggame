How to Play

Find all the matching pairs to complete the round.

  • Tap any two cards to reveal them.
  • If they match, they stay open. If not, try to remember them.
  • Match every pair to finish the round and earn points.
  • The first round uses emojis; later rounds use fresh two-digit numbers.

Congratulations!
